The wake word is working.

"Hey Hearty" — spoken at a normal volume, from across the room — now reliably wakes the app. The model was trained entirely on synthetic speech data, no recordings of my own voice required, and it's holding up well. This was one of the pieces I was most unsure about, and it's largely done.

## Why this matters so much to me personally

I'm building Hearty for myself. That's where it starts.

I have a genuinely hard time remembering to log my meals consistently. It's not that I don't care — I do. But there's always something else happening, and by the time I think about it, the window has passed. I can't tell you how many food journals I've started and abandoned.

And the apps I've tried don't help. Most of them ask you to fill out a lot of fields. Calories, macros, portion sizes, meal names. It takes a meaningful amount of time out of your day, every day. That's not sustainable for me.

I figured: if I'm struggling with this, other people probably are too. The problem isn't motivation — it's friction. The solution isn't a better form. It's removing the form entirely.

That's what the wake word is for. You say "Hey Hearty, had a bowl of pasta" while you're still at the table, and you're done. No unlocking the phone, no navigating to an app, no fields to fill out. The moment the thought occurs to you is the moment it gets logged.
