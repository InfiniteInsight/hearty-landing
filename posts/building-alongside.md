One of the quieter things about building an indie app is how easy it is to do it in complete isolation. You ship in private, you worry in private, and you have no idea whether anyone else is making the same mistakes or figuring out the same things.

This year I joined a small cohort of independent makers — people building their own tools and projects, mostly for audiences that bigger companies aren't thinking about. We share progress, give each other feedback, and occasionally talk each other down from bad decisions.

A few of them are building things I genuinely think are worth knowing about.

## Who they are

**Feygon** is building [SensoryNav](https://feygon.github.com/SensoryNav/) — a sensory-accommodation layer for maps, designed for neurodivergent and sensory-sensitive people. Standard map apps aren't built with them in mind. Feygon is fixing that.

**Sammie** runs [Disabled Fashion](https://gothbabeclothes.square.site/) — adaptive gothic clothing made by and for disabled people. It's the kind of project that shouldn't be niche but somehow is.

**Effy** is behind the [Veska Index](https://www.veskaindex.com/), a blind-observation research instrument for celestial timing systems. It's a bit harder to explain in one sentence, but if you're someone who tracks time by the sky, you'll get it immediately.

And **Molly** is making [Cat Apartment Simulator](https://cat-game-xi.vercel.app/) — a cozy 3D game where you play as a cat inside a real apartment, watching the humans' routines unfold. Low stakes, high charm.

## Why I'm featuring them

These aren't paid placements or reciprocal deals. They're just projects I think are good, made by people I've gotten to know through building in the same space.

There's also something thematically relevant here. Hearty exists because the tools available for tracking food and symptoms weren't built for people like me. Most of the projects in our cohort exist for the same reason — there's a gap, and someone decided to fill it themselves.

The Spotlights page is my small way of pointing at that work. If any of them are relevant to you, go check them out.
